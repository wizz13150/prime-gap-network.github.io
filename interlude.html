02 / Patience · the wide frame

Make room
for time.

The scene moves slowly. You choose when to leave it. Nothing here is a countdown.

03 / Checking · the detail

Look closer.
Check the mark.

A mark becomes a claim only after the check. Pause the rush and inspect what is actually there.

The camera settles on two engraved signs, close enough to compare.

Use the shot buttons at your own pace, or play the sequence. Arrow keys work within the shot controls. Motion can be paused; reduced-motion preferences start with still frames. No mining or PGN network work is performed.
