Primes never run out. Their spacing keeps changing. Finding and verifying unusual gaps turns that irregularity into data we can study.

01 / UNDERSTAND

What is a prime gap?

Take two consecutive primes. Subtract the smaller from the larger. Then ask what makes that distance unusual for its location.

Computation meets number theory

The interesting part
is the evidence.

A large gap is a finding. A first occurrence is a stronger claim. Proving that distinction requires trustworthy endpoints, a prime-free interior, and the right search coverage.

We keep candidates, proved gaps, and verified coverage distinct — and link back to the people and sources doing the work.

Learn what verification means ↗

Two paths.
Different responsibilities.

Use the standalone tool today, or help develop the proposed network. They are separate projects with separate workflows.

Standalone software

Prime Gap Scout

NVIDIA CUDA search software with resumable local work and reports. Its optimized arithmetic engines cover 67–95-bit ranges. No wallet or blockchain connection is needed.

Explore Scout ↗
Experimental network

PGN / Horizon

A testbed for coordinated work, audits, durable pending states, and reward-epoch design. Consensus and economic rules are still being designed and tested.

There is no production launch, live coin, or production explorer to join from this website.
